Showcase behavior

The showcase loads no remote font, analytics, advertising, social embed, or third-party runtime asset and sets no cookie. Models and Documents you select are read and processed locally in your browser; the showcase does not upload them.

A fresh visit does not register a service worker or write Web Storage or Cache Storage. If you explicitly choose Enable offline use, the showcase registers its service worker and stores only the built-in application shell, examples, and integrity-verified model sources in Cache Storage. It does not store files you selected. You can remove that data through your browser's site-data controls.
